2️⃣ PIZZA BEATS – 3D Endless Runner Game

Platform: WebGL Engine: Unity + C# Role: Lead Developer & 3D Artist
🎮 Game Link 🎥 Game Video

Fun endless scooty driving game.

🛠️ About the Game

Pizza Beats is a 3D endless runner game where the player controls a pizza delivery girl riding a scooter through a musical highway, collecting ingredients to fulfill dynamic pizza orders.

I proposed and led the complete development of this game — from concept to deployment — taking ownership of both technical and creative execution.

💻 Responsibilities

💻 Built all gameplay & system programming from scratch
🎨 Created all 3D models and environment assets
🎮 Implemented game mechanics and UI logic
📦 Managed WebGL deployment and optimization

🎮 Game Mechanics

The delivery girl rides through a music-driven highway while a music box drops pizza ingredients synchronized with the beat.

Players collect ingredients based on pending pizza orders shown in the top-right corner.

Once all required ingredients are collected, the order is completed and rewards points.

Extra Points Awarded For

  • 🍕 Completing more pizza orders
  • ⏱️ Completing orders quickly
  • 🧀 Collecting extra ingredients

🛍️ Shop System

Players can visit the in-game shop to buy:

  • 🛵 New scooters (visual + speed variants)
  • 💊 Revive potions (used after collisions)

💀 Game Over & Scoring

The run ends when the player collides with obstacles such as other vehicles.

Players can revive using a potion or continue directly to the Game Over screen.

Final Score Includes

  • 🍕 Number of orders completed
  • ⏱️ Total run time
  • 🧀 Extra ingredients collected

Players can also watch a rewarded ad to boost their final score.
