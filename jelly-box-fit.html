🍬 Jelly Box Fit : Yummy Journey

Jelly Box Fit : Yummy Journey is a fun and satisfying hyper-casual puzzle game I created for Android and mobile devices, designed to give players a quick burst of fun anytime, anywhere!

It is available on my Play Store account.

🍬 What Is the Game About?

Your task is simple yet surprisingly engaging:

👉 You are given a set of cute, colorful jelly pieces.

👉 Your goal is to fit all those jellies perfectly inside a box.

👉 Once the box is filled correctly, it closes — and the level is complete!

It’s a smooth and delightful gameplay loop that combines puzzle thinking with the satisfying feel of watching everything fit perfectly into place.
