🏙️ CITY OF BLUETOPIA

City Of Bluetopia is a hybrid casual football-themed game that I am currently developing independently.

The story follows a young boy who dreams of becoming a football legend but does not have enough money to buy football equipment.

To achieve his dream, he participates in different mini-games and challenges to earn money, slowly buying football gear and improving his skills — eventually becoming a football legend.

🎮 About The Game

City Of Bluetopia combines story progression, football-inspired gameplay, and hybrid casual mechanics to create an engaging experience.

Players progress from a struggling kid with limited resources to becoming a celebrated football superstar.

The game focuses on progression, rewards, skill growth, and fun gameplay loops.
